Makes the Darktide Mod Framework (DMF) options menu transparent with adjustable background opacity and blur controls.

Custom Mouse Cursors:
Requires SimpleAssets. Place a 48x48 or 64x64 PNG named cursor.png in TransparentModSettings/cursor_1/, cursor_2/, or cursor_3/.

Recommended:
- [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/warhammer40kdarktide/mods/329]PauseVision[/url] (for the escape menu)

[b]Options:[/b]

[b]General Settings[/b]
- Background Opacity (%)
- Emblem Opacity (%)
- Game World Blur
- Custom Mouse Cursor [Default, Cursor 1, Cursor 2, Cursor 3] (Requires SimpleAssets)
